# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Capricorn is a community project. We want participating — in issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other project space — to be respectful, welcoming
and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or any
other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback about _work_, not people.
- Respect differing opinions and experience levels.
- Credit others' work, including upstream authors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks; trolling or deliberate derailing.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code and
may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe
violations.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the repository's private
reporting channel (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach them
privately). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in
an incident recuse themselves from handling it.
